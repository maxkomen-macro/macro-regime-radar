The regime tag stays Overheating, but the 39% Stagflation weight is doing more work than the headline confidence suggests. Q1 GDP at 2.0% and a widening March trade deficit confirm domestic demand is still running hot, and the 27bp monthly jump in the 10Y to 4.67% — with Fed Funds unchanged at 3.64% — is a pure term-premium move, not a growth repricing. That's the tell: the bond market is digesting sticky inflation and energy risk, not a soft landing. Today's tape reinforces the regime call.

The tension is the 2s10s steepening (+2bp) into a VIX uptick and an energy shock. Normally a bear steepener with rising vol screams late-cycle inflation panic, but credit and equities aren't cracking. Either the curve is right and we're early in repricing higher-for-longer, or risk assets are complacent about the oil pass-through.

What's priced: a Fed on extended hold, with cuts pushed out and the BOJ converging higher on 4.9% Japanese PPI. What's at risk: a second leg in crude that forces the conversation from "hold" back to "hike," collapsing the steepener and the equity multiple together.

Watch the 10Y auction tail and breakevens for term-premium continuation, any Fed speaker entertaining additional tightening, and Brent above recent range highs. BOJ June pricing is the offshore tell.
