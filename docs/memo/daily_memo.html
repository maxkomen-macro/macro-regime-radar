◆ CLAUDE OPUS 4.7 · ADAPTIVE THINKING

The regime read stays Overheating with 83% model conviction, and today's tape reinforces it rather than challenges it. The Fed signaling a possible 2026 hike — not cut — is the tell: growth is firm enough and inflation sticky enough that the committee is walking back the easing path the curve had embedded. Fed funds at 3.63 and 10Y at 4.43 with 2s10s steepening modestly off the lows is consistent with a market belatedly accepting "higher for longer" rather than pricing imminent recession. Stagflation at 17% is the only meaningful alternative; Goldilocks and Recession are rounding errors.

The interesting tension is VIX up three vol points month-over-month while Kospi prints record highs and U.S. futures grind up. Risk appetite and rate-vol are rising together, which usually doesn't last — either equity vol catches down as earnings carry the tape, or equities catch down to the rates repricing. Citi's point that returns are earnings-led, not multiple-led, is what's keeping the first outcome alive for now.

What's priced: a Fed that stays restrictive through 2026 with a shallow, data-dependent path. What's at risk: the front end cheapening another 15–25bp if upcoming inflation or labor prints come in hot and the 2026 hike gets pulled forward.

Watch the next core PCE print and any Warsh follow-through on guidance posture; 2-year yield above 4.20 confirms the repricing has legs.
